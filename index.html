About this data

Sources

  • Ward boundaries: Survey Department, Government of Nepal (National Geoportal)
  • Rivers: HydroRIVERS v1.0 (HydroSHEDS)
  • River and rain monitors: BIPAD portal (bipadportal.gov.np), cross-checked with the DHM station list (hydrology.gov.np)
  • Markets: OpenStreetMap contributors (Overpass), including features named bazaar, haat and mandi
  • Flood hazard map: METEOR project (maps.meteor-project.org), modelled with Fathom, licensed ODbL. It shows modelled water depth in metres for a flood of the chosen rarity. It is loaded live from METEOR, so it needs internet
  • Base maps: OpenStreetMap contributors; satellite imagery: Esri

Method: a place is listed if the river downstream of a monitor passes through it, up to 25 km along the river, for monitors that reported within the last 48 hours.

For maintainers: the filters can only narrow the pre-computed data in data/. To go further, change MAX_RIVER_KM / ACTIVE_WINDOW_H in the pipeline scripts and re-export.
